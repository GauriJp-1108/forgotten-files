📁 The Case File

The Ashford Diamond Necklace has been in the Ashford family for nearly 100 years.

It is one of the family's most valuable possessions.

The necklace is normally kept inside a locked glass display cabinet in the study.

It isn't something that can simply be picked up and hidden in a pocket.

And tonight, it was supposed to be shown to the guests.

03

🚨 The Discovery

At approximately 8:43 PM, Eleanor returned to the study.

The cabinet was still there.

The lock wasn't damaged.

!
THE NECKLACE WAS GONE.

No broken glass. No obvious signs of forced entry.

Eleanor immediately called everyone into the study.

Nobody was allowed to leave the house.

The police arrived 25 minutes later.

There were only five people who could realistically have taken the necklace.

And every single one of them had something suspicious about their story.
