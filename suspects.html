CASE #001 • PERSONS OF INTEREST

The People In The House

Five people were inside Ashford Manor during the disappearance of the necklace. Each had access, opportunity, or a reason to attract suspicion. But suspicion is not proof.

SUSPECT 01
EA

Eleanor Ashford

Owner of Ashford Manor

AGE 48
RELATIONSHIP Owner

Eleanor is the owner of the Ashford Diamond Necklace. She has lived at Ashford Manor for years and knows exactly where the family's valuables are kept.

STATEMENT

"I showed everyone the necklace at around 8:20. I locked it away and went back to dinner."

WHY SUSPICIOUS?

She owns the necklace and was the person who handled the cabinet immediately before it disappeared.

SUSPECT 02
DA

Daniel Ashford

Eleanor's Son

AGE 24
RELATIONSHIP Son

Daniel recently returned home after several months away. He knows Ashford Manor extremely well and has grown up around the family's possessions.

STATEMENT

"I went upstairs around 8:15. I had an important phone call."

WHY SUSPICIOUS?

Daniel has been dealing with financial problems and knows the house better than most guests.

SUSPECT 03
MR

Marcus Reed

Family Friend

AGE 41
RELATIONSHIP Family Friend

Marcus has known the Ashford family for years. He was invited to dinner and was familiar with the layout of the manor.

STATEMENT

"I stayed in the living room. Around 8:30, I stepped outside for some fresh air."

WHY SUSPICIOUS?

Marcus admits he was outside during part of the critical time period.

SUSPECT 04
CW

Clara Wilson

Housekeeper

AGE 39
RELATIONSHIP Employee

Clara has worked at Ashford Manor for eight years. She knows the rooms, the family's routines and where valuable possessions are kept.

STATEMENT

"I was in the kitchen preparing the dessert. I heard someone walking toward the study around 8:30."

WHY SUSPICIOUS?

Clara has unrestricted familiarity with the house and could move around without attracting attention.

SUSPECT 05
TB

Thomas Blake

Private Chef

AGE 46
RELATIONSHIP Chef

Thomas was hired to prepare the dinner. He spent most of the evening in the kitchen and claims he never left.

STATEMENT

"I didn't leave the kitchen once."

WHY SUSPICIOUS?

The kitchen has a back door leading outside, giving Thomas an opportunity to move around the property.

DETECTIVE'S NOTE

Every suspect has a reason to be questioned.

That does not mean every suspect is guilty.

Keep their statements in mind. You will need them later.

YOU HAVE MET THE SUSPECTS.
