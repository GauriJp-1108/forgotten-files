Eleanor Ashford

Owner of Ashford Manor · Age 48
PROFILE

Eleanor Ashford is the owner of Ashford Manor and the keeper of the Ashford Diamond Necklace. She has lived in the manor for more than twenty years and knows its rooms, locks and routines intimately.

WITNESS STATEMENT

"I showed the necklace to everyone at around 8:20. It was something of a tradition whenever we had guests. I remember opening the cabinet myself."

"After everyone had seen it, I closed the cabinet and made sure it was locked. I then returned to the dining room. I had no reason to think anything was wrong."

"The necklace belonged to my family long before I inherited the house. I understand why people might think I would benefit from losing it, but that necklace means far more to me than its insurance value."

"The camera going down was strange. I assumed it was some kind of technical problem. The system has never been completely reliable."

👨🏻

Daniel Ashford

Eleanor's Son · Age 24
PROFILE

Daniel is Eleanor's only son. He has recently been struggling financially and has occasionally argued with his mother about money. He knows the manor well and has spent most of his childhood there.

WITNESS STATEMENT

"I went upstairs around 8:15. I had an important phone call to deal with and didn't want everyone listening in."

"I stayed upstairs for most of the evening. I remember hearing people moving around downstairs, but I wasn't paying attention to what they were doing."

"Yes, I knew Mother was showing the necklace. Everyone knew about it. It's practically the first thing people ask about when they visit."

"I didn't go anywhere near the study. I certainly didn't touch the cabinet. I wouldn't even know why I'd need to open it."

"When I came back downstairs, everyone seemed confused. Mother was already talking about the missing necklace. That's when I realized something had happened."

👨🏻‍💼

Marcus Reed

Family Friend · Age 41
PROFILE

Marcus Reed has known the Ashford family for more than fifteen years. He is a frequent guest at the manor and is familiar with the property, although he does not live there.

WITNESS STATEMENT

"I stayed in the living room after dinner. Eleanor was talking with Clara, and Thomas was still working in the kitchen."

"Around 8:30, I stepped outside for some fresh air. It was only for a few minutes. I remember the ground was wet because of the rain earlier."

"I didn't enter the study. I have no reason to go in there unless Eleanor specifically asks me to."

"When I came back inside, everyone was still where I expected them to be. I didn't notice anything unusual."

"The necklace is beautiful, certainly, but I have never had any interest in owning it. Eleanor knows that."

👩🏻‍🍳

Clara Wilson

Housekeeper · Age 39
PROFILE

Clara has worked at Ashford Manor for nearly eight years. She manages much of the household routine and knows which rooms are occupied, where valuables are stored and how the house is arranged.

WITNESS STATEMENT

"I was in the kitchen preparing dessert. There was still quite a bit to do before it could be served."

"At around 8:30 I heard a door close somewhere along the hallway. I couldn't tell exactly who it was. There were people moving around that evening."

"I know the study well. I clean it regularly, but I wouldn't go inside without a reason while guests are visiting."

"The camera system is old. I've seen it lose its signal before, although I don't know why it happened that night."

"I didn't see anyone carrying the necklace. If someone took it, they would have had to be very careful."

👨🏻‍🍳

Thomas Blake

Private Chef · Age 46
PROFILE

Thomas is the Ashford family's private chef. He has worked for Eleanor for three years and was responsible for preparing dessert and finishing the evening meal. He had access to the kitchen and its back entrance.

WITNESS STATEMENT

"I didn't leave the kitchen. I was finishing the dessert and keeping an eye on the oven."

"There was no reason for me to go into the study. Eleanor had already shown everyone the necklace before I returned to preparing the meal."

"The kitchen has a back door, yes. But I didn't use it. I had everything I needed inside."

"I did notice the weather had made the garden muddy. I had been outside earlier in the afternoon to bring supplies inside."

"When Eleanor announced that the necklace was gone, everyone immediately started looking at one another. I had nothing to hide."

INVESTIGATOR'S NOTE

Statements are not evidence by themselves. Compare what each person says with the crime scene, physical evidence and timeline.

Pay particular attention to details that appear insignificant. A person does not always lie by inventing a story — sometimes they lie by leaving something out.
