CASE #001 • CRIME SCENE

The Study

Ashford Manor • Saturday, October 18

🔐

The Display Cabinet

The Ashford Diamond Necklace was normally kept inside a locked glass display cabinet in the study.

When investigators arrive, the cabinet is still standing exactly where it was.

  • No broken glass.
  • No scratches around the lock.
  • No visible signs of forced entry.
  • The cabinet itself is completely intact.
OBSERVATION Whoever opened the cabinet may not have needed to force it open.
🪟

The Study Window

The study window is found open when Eleanor discovers that the necklace is missing.

At first glance, the open window appears to suggest that someone entered or escaped from outside.

  • No scratches on the window frame.
  • No fingerprints of interest.
  • No dirt on the inner sill.
  • No damaged plants beneath the window.
OBSERVATION The window may not have been the thief's entrance or escape route.
👞

The Muddy Footprint

A single muddy men's shoe print is discovered beside the study window.

SIZE US 10
TYPE Formal leather shoe
LOCATION Beside study window

Marcus Reed wears size 10 shoes and had apparently stepped outside for fresh air.

However, investigators notice something unusual.

QUESTION If the footprint came from someone climbing through the window, why is the windowsill completely clean?
📹

The Hallway Camera

A security camera covers the hallway outside the study.

8:24 PM

Eleanor is seen walking toward the dining room.

8:25 PM

SIGNAL LOST

8:40 PM

Camera signal is restored.

OBSERVATION The camera was offline for exactly fifteen minutes.
🍷

The Dining Room

Investigators inspect the dining table after Eleanor claims everyone returned to dinner.

🍷 Eleanor
🍷 Marcus
🍷 Daniel
🥛 Clara

The glasses are still partially full. The table does not look as though everyone remained seated continuously.

OBSERVATION Someone may have left the dining area during the critical period.
🍰

The Kitchen

Thomas Blake claims he remained in the kitchen preparing dessert throughout the evening.

Investigators find an unfinished cake and an oven still running.

But the kitchen also has a back door leading outside.

QUESTION Could the kitchen have provided someone with another route through the house?
🔑

The Spare Key

Eleanor keeps a spare key for the display cabinet in a drawer inside her bedroom.

Investigators search the drawer.

🔑 KEY FOUND Still inside Eleanor's bedroom drawer.
OBSERVATION The spare key does not appear to have been taken.
🕵️

INVESTIGATOR'S NOTE

The scene initially suggests an outside intruder. The open window and muddy footprint seem convincing.

But physical evidence does not completely support that theory.

Examine every detail carefully. Some evidence may be exactly what someone wanted investigators to find.
