CASE #001

The Solution

DA
THE ACTUAL THIEF

Daniel Ashford

24 · Eleanor Ashford's son

01
HOW DID HE DO IT?

He was already inside.

Daniel did not need to enter through the window or use the kitchen entrance. He was already inside Ashford Manor as one of the guests.

After the necklace was displayed, Daniel used his knowledge of the study and the cabinet to take the necklace. He then opened the study window and staged the scene to make it appear that someone had entered from outside.

The muddy footprint near the window supported the false story, but the clean windowsill and untouched flowerbed showed that the window was not the real entry point.

02
THE CABINET

The lock was the hidden clue.

The display cabinet showed no signs of forced entry. That meant the thief either had a key or understood how the locking mechanism worked.

Daniel had grown up in the manor and had watched Eleanor use the cabinet many times.

The cabinet had a subtle flaw: if the key was turned slightly past the locking position, the cabinet could appear locked even though the latch had not fully engaged.

Daniel knew this and used it to make the theft look like an impossible locked-room crime.

03
THE CAMERA

He created a gap in the timeline.

The hallway security camera stopped recording at approximately 8:25 PM and returned at 8:40 PM.

This gave Daniel enough time to move around the house without appearing on camera.

His familiarity with the manor and its security system made the camera interruption far more significant than it first appeared.

04
THE FALSE ALIBI

His phone call did not cover the whole disappearance.

Daniel claimed that he was upstairs on an important phone call from around 8:15 until 8:40.

The phone records told a different story. The actual call began at approximately 8:37 PM and ended at 8:44 PM.

That left a significant gap between 8:20 and 8:37 PM — exactly when the necklace disappeared.

05
WHY DID HE DO IT?

Financial pressure.

Daniel had been struggling financially and had argued with Eleanor about money.

The necklace was worth approximately ₹2.4 crore. Taking it gave Daniel access to an enormous amount of potential value.

His plan was not simply to steal the necklace. He wanted to make the theft look like an outside intrusion, while his phone call created a false impression that he had been upstairs.

THE FINAL CONNECTION

The most suspicious clue was not the most important clue.

The muddy footprint pointed toward Marcus. The kitchen access made Thomas suspicious. Clara knew the manor. Eleanor controlled the necklace.

But Daniel's incomplete alibi, his knowledge of the manor, the cabinet mechanism, the camera interruption and the staged window all fit together.

Daniel Ashford stole the Ashford Diamond Necklace.
